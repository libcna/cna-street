# Before and after, the seventh pass

Every "before" is the frame committed at `9cec90d`, the end of the sixth
pass, at the size it was committed at; every "after" is the same viewpoint
on this pass's final build. Pairs are at one sample so what they show is
the scene and not the sampling; the flagship frames are resolved at two.

## The one that proves it

**`pairs/district-behind-the-frontage-before.png` /
`-after.png`** -- a camera forty-five metres up over the north arm,
looking back across the west blocks. Before: one row of buildings, then a
checkerboard of grass and dirt to the horizon with a haze of skyline
blocks 240 m out. After: two rows of blocks behind the frontage, a service
lane between each, pitched roofs with stacks and flat ones with plant,
thinning into the skyline scatter. This is the view the sixth pass never
took, and the reason the street read as a set from any window above the
second floor.

**`before/06-above-the-junction.png` / `after/06-above-the-junction.png`**
-- the same defect from the project's own flagship viewpoint, which had
been in the README since the first pass with the field in it.

## The people in the cars

**`lineup/driver-before.png` / `lineup/driver-after.png`** -- the
saloon's driver's window in `--lineup`. Before: a grey ellipsoid with no
face and two spheres for hands, in the passenger seat, with the steering
wheel beside it and nobody behind it. After: one of the crowd's own
figures, on the driver's side, hands on the rim.
**`lineup/driver-after-yellow-car.png`** is the same for the car whose
wheels used to wobble.

## The street, before and after

`before/` and `after/` carry five of the eighteen named viewpoints. Every
one of them changed, because the traffic now keeps right: the parked cars
on each side face the other way, the moving cars are in the other lane,
and the signal heads stand on the other kerb. Look for:

* **`07-the-long-view-south`** -- the far end of the street. The district's
  rows are visible between and over the frontage where there was sky
  and field; the shadow under the near tree is the same tree's shadow
  drawn from a quarter of the triangles.
* **`16-corner-to-corner`** -- the city behind the corner block, upper
  left and upper right.
* **`09-car-three-metres`** -- the Punto parked on the right, facing
  away, as a car parked on the right of a right-hand-traffic street does;
  its contact shadow, from the far copy, is the same shape.
* **`01-footway-looking-south-to-the-junction`** -- no change wanted here
  and none made, except the cars.

## The frames

`flagship/mid-street-north.png` -- a camera at eye height on the
carriageway, ninety-five metres south of the junction, looking north
through the whole modelled street to where the district takes over. Two
samples.

## What did not change

The sixth pass's six live-play invariants -- forward-facing traffic,
plausible legs, uncollapsed crowds, collision with cars and furniture,
visible drivers, no lofts in the hero bays -- are all still there, and
`--walkthrough` and the test suites say so in numbers rather than in
pictures; see `report.md`.
